# SEO Skill

Refer to the full specification and audit workflow in [seo-optimization](file:///d:/Joyce/Freelance/Website-Ecommerce-NhuaThuanDat/.agents/skills/seo-optimization/SKILL.md).

## Quick Checklist

1. **Title & Description**: Unique per page, 50-60 chars title, 140-160 chars meta description.
2. **Canonical**: Self-referencing canonical URL on every page.
3. **OpenGraph & Twitter**: `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`, `twitter:card`.
4. **Structured Data**: Schema.org JSON-LD for `Organization`, `WebSite`, `Product`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `NewsArticle`.
5. **Sitemap**: Valid `sitemap.xml` adhering to sitemaps.org & Google Image sitemap schema with accurate W3C `lastmod`.
6. **Semantic HTML**: Exactly one `h1`, proper `h2`/`h3` hierarchy, meaningful `alt` text on images.
